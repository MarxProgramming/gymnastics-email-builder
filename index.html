?Use your script in Google Sheets＋
  1. Check your headings.

    Your sheet needs columns for the child’s name, parent’s name and email address, in any order. The script finds the heading row within the first 20 rows and recognises common variations such as Child Name, Guardian Name and Email Address. Other columns can stay.

    Childs NameParents NameEmail

    An Email Status column is added at the right when you send, if it’s missing. A Review column is optional.

  2. Paste your script.

    Open Extensions → Apps Script in your sheet. In a new project, replace the starter code with your copied script, save, then reload the sheet. Use one club script per project; take care to preserve any existing scripts.

  3. Select your rows and send.

    Highlight the recipient rows you want, then choose your club’s email menu → Send selected emails. It sends straight away to each eligible selected row, one at a time, and refreshes the result in Email Status. Existing status text never blocks a send. Google may ask you to authorise the script the first time.

A few helpful details

Use Check selected rows (no email) first to see which headings were found and how many selected rows are ready to send. This check sends nothing and leaves your sheet unchanged.

Selecting a previously sent row sends it another email. You can reuse your sheet and leave old Email Status values in place for a new message.

Rows with anything in Review are skipped when that column is present. Hidden and filtered rows, missing names and invalid email addresses are also skipped. Google filter views are not detected. Siblings sharing an address each get their own email.

If a send is interrupted or an error appears, check Sent mail before selecting that row again. If Google’s allowance or the time limit is reached, select only the remaining unsent rows to continue.

Emails come from the Google account running the script. Use the appropriate club account.
